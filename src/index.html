    About PCB Beach Flags

    Panama City Beach flies color-coded flags to show current water hazards. Only one forum recorded this data before, with no way to visualize it, so I created this tool.

    Consistent data collection started 2/28/2025. How the data is collected.

    • Green Flag: Low hazard, calm conditions, safe for swimming.
    • Yellow Flag: Medium hazard, moderate surf and currents – use caution.
    • Red Flag: High hazard, strong currents, dangerous conditions – swimming not advised.
    • Double Red Flag: Water closed to public use due to extreme hazards.
    • Purple Flag: Dangerous marine life present (e.g., jellyfish, sharks).
    • Yellow Over Purple Flag: Medium surf hazard plus dangerous marine life.
    • Red Over Purple Flag: High surf hazard plus dangerous marine life.

    Always follow the flags posted at the beach.
